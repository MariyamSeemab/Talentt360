🎥

Record your child's 1-minute speaking video following all the rules, then submit it using the entry form below.

✅

Video Guidelines & Rules

Official Rules for a Fair & Exciting Challenge

Please read all the rules carefully before recording your video.

⏱️
Min 30 secondsMinimum duration
🎯
Max 60 secondsMaximum duration
🎬

1. One-Shot Video

Record in a single take. No cuts, retakes or joining of clips allowed.

✂️

2. No Editing

Video editing is not allowed in any form.

🚫

3. No Filters

No beauty filters, face filters or video effects allowed.

🔇

4. No Background Music

No background music, sound effects or added audio allowed.

🧍

5. Full Body Visible

Participant's head-to-toe full body must be clearly visible.

👨‍👩‍👧

6. Parent / Guardian Recording

Parent or guardian may hold the phone. Mobile stand or tripod is not compulsory.

📱

7. Vertical Video

Record in vertical (9:16) portrait format only.

💡

8. Clear Audio & Video

Face must be clearly visible and voice clearly audible. Record in a quiet place.

🎤

9. One Topic Only

Select one topic and speak only on that topic.

🗣️

10. Natural Speech

Speech must be in the student's own voice and natural presentation.

🙌

11. Expressions & Gestures

Use gestures, posture and facial expressions naturally.

👀

12. Look at the Camera

Look into the camera and speak confidently.

⚠️ Important: Videos that do not follow the rules may be disqualified from the competition.
